💡 What Is cloudflare-os?

cloudflare-os is a workspace application that runs on your Windows computer and connects to the Cloudflare Workers platform. In plain terms, it is a single place where you can write and organise documents, put together small working apps, and set up automated helpers — often called agents — that know about your company’s own context and systems.

Instead of juggling several separate tools, you open cloudflare-os and everything sits side by side. You describe what you want in everyday language, and the workspace helps you turn that description into a real document or a real working app. The background technology handles the complicated parts, so you can focus on the result.

Because the workspace is built on Cloudflare’s global network, the pages and apps you create stay quick to open and available wherever your team happens to be. You do not need to manage servers, install web frameworks, or understand how hosting works. You install the app, sign in, and start creating.

💻 Before You Begin

Please check that your computer meets these everyday requirements. They describe a typical modern Windows setup.

  • Operating system: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
  • Processor: A dual-core processor or better.
  • Memory: 4 GB of RAM or more.
  • Storage: At least 500 MB of free space for the application and your working files.
  • Display: A screen resolution of 1280 × 720 or higher.
  • Internet connection: A stable connection is required, because the workspace connects to Cloudflare services while you work.
  • Account: A Cloudflare account with access to your organisation’s workspace.
  • Permissions: You should be able to install software on your computer. If you are on a company laptop, ask your IT team first.

🧭 Everyday Use

The workspace is organised around three kinds of work, and you can move between them freely.

Documents

Use documents for anything written: meeting notes, proposals, internal policies, product descriptions, and project summaries. You can start from a blank page or from an existing document that already matches your company’s style. Copying an existing document is often the fastest route, because it preserves the headings and wording your colleagues expect.

Apps

Apps are small tools that do one job well. A typical example is a form that collects requests from colleagues and shows them in a list. Another is a simple dashboard that displays a handful of key numbers. You build these by describing what you want and adjusting the result, rather than by writing code.

Agents

Agents are assistants you configure once and reuse. You tell an agent what information it may use and what kind of task it should help with, and it then handles that task repeatedly. A common pattern is an agent that drafts a first version of a routine document so a person can review and refine it.

🛠️ If Something Goes Wrong

The download does not start

Refresh the download page and try again. If your browser blocks the download, look for a small notice near the address bar and allow the file. Try a different browser if the problem continues.

Windows shows a security prompt

This is normal for software downloaded from the internet. Check that you downloaded from the official releases page, then choose the option to continue.

The application opens but will not sign in

Confirm your internet connection is working and that your account has access to your organisation’s workspace. Closing the app and opening it again resolves most sign-in hiccups.

The application feels slow

Check whether other programs are using a lot of memory. Closing unused browser tabs and restarting your computer often restores normal speed.

You cannot install software

Some company laptops restrict installations. Contact your IT team and ask them to allow the application, or to install it for you.

The instructions on this page are written for Windows computers. Other platforms may be supported in the releases list, but the steps here assume Windows.

Yes. The workspace connects to Cloudflare services while you work, so keep a stable connection available.

How do I get a newer version?

🔒 Privacy and Your Data

cloudflare-os connects to services that run on Cloudflare’s network. Your workspace content belongs to your organisation, and access is controlled by the account you sign in with. If you are unsure whether a particular piece of information may be placed in the workspace, ask your manager or your IT team before adding it.
